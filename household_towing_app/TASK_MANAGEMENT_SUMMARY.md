**Project**: DUALSERVE - Household Towing Service App

**Tech Stack**:
- Framework: Flutter/Dart
- Backend: Firebase (Firestore, Authentication)
- Deployment: Web, Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, macOS

**Modules Completed**:
1. Authentication (Login/Register with Google Sign-in)
2. Customer Module (Booking, Tracking)
3. Provider Module (Schedule, Tracking, Booking Requests)
4. Admin Dashboard (Analytics, Management)
5. **Task and Schedule Management** (newly built)

**Current Work**: Building Task and Schedule Management Module to enable efficient job assignment, scheduling, and availability management across providers and admins.
